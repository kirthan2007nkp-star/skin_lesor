Folder names MUST remain exactly:
  benign
  melanoma

For a meaningful project, use a reputable labeled dataset and follow its
license/terms. A commonly used research source is the ISIC Challenge data:
https://challenge.isic-archive.com/data/

Do not use random unlabeled images from the internet as training data.

The included training script requires at least 10 images per class only to
verify that the pipeline can run. A serious experiment needs substantially
more properly labeled data and careful validation.
